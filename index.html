Cryptnox Shamir

cryptnox-shamir splits a single master secret — a Bitcoin treasury key, an HSM wrapping key, a recovery seed — across n tamper-resistant smart cards, of which any k are enough to reconstruct it. No single card, and no single person, ever holds the secret. It is built for institutional custody: corporate Bitcoin treasuries, funds, exchanges, and any organisation that needs verifiable, quorum-based control over a high-value cryptographic secret.
